# Music credits

All tracks are **placeholders** (PLAN.md open question 2): short synth loops
built at runtime by `Sound._synth()` in `autoload/sound.gd` from the recipes in
`Sound.TRACKS`. No audio files ship, so they add nothing to the web build.
Original to this project, no license restrictions.

Replace a placeholder by dropping `<track>.ogg` (loop on import, 60-90 s,
about 2 MB) here and recording its source and license in the table.
